Made to move

A big world on Mac. A light touch in your hand.

EarthGuide3D displays the immersive 3D world on your Mac. Your phone or tablet becomes a wireless controller, so you can search, select places, and guide the journey without crowding the view.

  • Run EarthGuide3D on a compatible Mac.
  • Connect a phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi network as a wireless controller.
  • For Sports Mode, install the free third-party Phyphox app separately on another mobile device to use as the motion sensor.
