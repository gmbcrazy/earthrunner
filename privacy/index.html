Privacy Policy

How EarthGuide3D handles information when you explore the world.

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to EarthGuide3D and the EarthRunner website. EarthGuide3D is an EarthRunner application for Mac. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@earthrunner.org.

Information We Handle

EarthGuide3D is designed to work without creating an EarthRunner account. Depending on the features you use, the app and its local wireless controller may handle the following information:

  • Places and guide requests: a destination you choose, related place information, and the settings needed to provide an AI guide response.
  • Controller and motion data: locally transmitted control input and motion-sensor readings used to operate the experience, including Sports Mode. EarthGuide3D does not use this information to identify you.
  • Optional settings: preferences and, if you deliberately choose an advanced bring-your-own-key option, that setting is kept in local app storage on your Mac.
  • Purchase information: when purchases are available, Apple processes payment. EarthGuide3D may receive transaction information needed to verify an entitlement or restore a purchase; it does not receive your full payment-card information.
  • Support messages: the information you include when you email us for support.

How Information Is Used

We use information only to operate and improve the requested feature: to show a selected place, connect a controller on your local network, process a motion input, provide an AI guide response, verify a purchase, respond to support, and protect the service from misuse.

EarthRunner does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

When Information Leaves Your Device

The wireless controller communicates with EarthGuide3D on your local network. Map imagery, place search, and online guide features require internet access. When you choose to use the AI guide, the selected place and request context may be sent through EarthRunner’s hosted service to generate the guide response and audio. The full local place database is not uploaded for this purpose.

Third-party services involved in the experience may include map-data providers, Apple for App Store transactions, and service providers used to deliver hosted AI features. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy terms.

Data Retention and Security

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain support records. App settings and local controller data are stored on your device unless a feature needs to send a request to an online service. We use reasonable measures to protect service data, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

You can choose not to use online map or AI-guide features, decline microphone permission for voice search, and avoid Sports Mode. You can remove local app settings by deleting the app’s data from your Mac. You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

Children

EarthGuide3D is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support message, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as EarthGuide3D develops. The latest version will always be available at this page, with its revision date shown above.
