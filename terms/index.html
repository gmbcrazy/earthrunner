Purchases and Subscriptions

Current products, prices, durations, and usage allowances are displayed in EarthGuide3D and confirmed by the Mac App Store before purchase. Apple processes payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund requests.

  • Adventure Pass: a one-time consumable purchase. Unused time and teleports remain available and additional Adventure Pass purchases add to the existing balance.
  • Explorer and Worldwalker: auto-renewing monthly subscriptions. Each plan’s time and teleport allowances apply to the current subscription period and unused subscription allowances do not roll over.

Subscriptions renew unless canceled through your Apple Account at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in the App Store. Restores, renewals, refunds, and access remain subject to Apple’s transaction records and policies.

EarthGuide3D relies on internet and third-party services for maps, imagery, place information, purchase verification, and optional AI guide features. Availability, coverage, accuracy, and response times can vary. Those services may change or become temporarily unavailable.

Map positions, place descriptions, narration, and AI-generated information are provided for exploration and general information. Do not rely on EarthGuide3D for navigation, emergencies, access rights, safety decisions, or professional advice.

Controller and Physical Safety

The browser controller and optional Sports Mode operate over your local network. Use a trusted private network or personal hotspot, keep the pairing QR code private, and remain aware of your physical surroundings. Do not use movement features near traffic, stairs, water, obstacles, unsafe edges, or anywhere that requires your full attention.
